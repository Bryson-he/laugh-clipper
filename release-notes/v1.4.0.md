## What's new

### Smoother, easier-to-read meters
- The meters now follow the sound in real time: loudness updates 20 times a second instead of 4, and every bar
  glides instead of jumping.
- A thin **peak marker** holds your highest recent score for a couple of seconds, so you can see where your laughs
  land and set the trigger level just below it.

### Calibrate for your game (optional)
- **Detection → Calibrate…** listens to your game for about a minute, then to you laughing, and suggests a trigger
  level that sits between the two: high enough to ignore the game's own sounds, low enough to catch your laughs.
- It shows the result on one bar, warns you if your laughs don't stand out from the game (with what to try
  instead), and gives other sounds the game makes a lot of, like cheering, their own level.
- Nothing changes until you click **Apply**, and you can still tune everything by hand.

## Fixes
- Disabled blue buttons now look disabled.
- Fixed a test that occasionally failed for no reason, so the automatic checks on GitHub stay reliable.

Your settings carry over.
