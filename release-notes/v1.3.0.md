## What's new

### Bookmark your recordings
- Recording a whole session in Medal? Turn on **Clipping → While Medal is recording → Add a bookmark instead of a
  clip**. While Medal is recording, a laugh then drops a bookmark into the recording instead of saving a separate
  clip.
- It notices recordings however you start them, and shows a red **Recording** badge on the Clipping page.
- The popup says "Medal is recording", its button becomes **Mark**, and it finishes with **Bookmarked**. Bookmarks
  show up in **History** too.

### Your hotkeys, read straight from Medal
- Laugh Clipper now reads your clip and bookmark hotkeys from Medal's settings, so you don't have to type them in.
- The Clipping page tells you whether your clip hotkey matches Medal's. If Medal has several clip lengths you can
  pick one from a menu; if it has one, it's a single button.
- If they can't be read, just click the key and press it. The bookmark hotkey can be set the same way.
- The setup guide fills both in for you, and warns if the clip hotkey is actually Medal's bookmark key.

### Notification sound
- An optional sound when the popup appears, handy in exclusive-fullscreen games where the popup can't show.
- Choose from **Soft chime**, **Bubble pop**, **Marimba** or **Bright bell**, set the volume, and pick which
  output it plays on (with Wave Link, choose a channel you can hear, like Game).
- Find it under **Notification → Sound**. It's off until you turn it on.

## Fixes
- **The meters looked empty.** They only showed the laugh score, which stays near zero for normal noise. Each meter
  now also shows how loud the sound is in grey, with the score on top in blue, turning green once it would trigger.
- **The meters stopped while you were setting things up** with "Only listen while playing" on, as soon as you
  clicked another monitor. Laugh Clipper now keeps listening whenever its settings window or setup guide is open.

Your settings carry over, and the new options are off until you turn them on.
