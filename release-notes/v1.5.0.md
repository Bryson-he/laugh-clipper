## What's new

### Longer clips for longer laughs
- If you've set up more than one clip length in Medal (like 30 s, 60 s and 120 s), turn on **Clipping → Pick the clip
  length to fit the moment**.
- Short laughs use your normal clip hotkey. Each time the laughing goes on longer than **Use a longer clip after
  laughing for** (8 s to start with, you can change it), it moves up to Medal's next clip length, so the start of a
  big moment isn't cut off.
- The "Clip saved" popup and the History page show which length was used.
- With only one clip length in Medal, these options are greyed out and tell you how to add more.
- The Clipping page also warns you if a moment can last longer than the clip Medal saves.

### What's new
- After an update, this window shows you what changed. Open it again any time from **General → What's New**.

## Fixes
- **Some microphones couldn't be used at all**, including Elgato XLR Dock mics and many headset and USB mics. They'd
  stop straight away with an unexplained error. They now work.

Your settings carry over, and the new options are off until you turn them on.
