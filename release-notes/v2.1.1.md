## What's new

This update is all about reliability.

### Fixes
- **"Clip didn't save" when it had.** Sometimes the popup and History said Medal didn't make a clip even though it
  was there. The check now always finds it.
- If Medal is just slow, Laugh Clipper keeps looking for another 30 seconds and corrects the popup and the History
  entry when the clip turns up.

### Everything working?
- The top of the **General** page now shows whether everything needed to save clips is in place: Medal running, your
  hotkey matching Medal's, the clips folder, and your audio. Anything wrong is listed with what to do about it.
- **Test Clip** presses Medal's hotkey for real and checks the clip appears.
- **General → Troubleshooting** has **Open Log** and **Copy Diagnostics**, a summary you can paste to whoever's
  helping. It leaves out folders and game names.

### Keeps itself going
- An audio source that goes quiet or hangs is restarted by itself, and the tray icon shows an orange dot with
  "Can't hear: …" if it stays missing.
- If you leave the microphone on **Windows default**, it follows along when you change the default.
- Hotkeys are put back after your PC wakes from sleep.
- If a game runs as administrator, Windows blocks the clip hotkey. Laugh Clipper now tells you when that's
  happening.
- Your settings are backed up each time they load properly, and restored if the file is ever damaged.

### Updates
- A failed update check tries again by itself instead of giving up.
- The downloaded installer is checked against the one published on GitHub before it runs.

### Lighter
- Starts about half a second faster and uses about 40 MB less memory, and the installer is a little smaller.
