## What's new

### Much lighter on your PC
- Laugh Clipper was making Windows' audio service work hard in the background: about 5% of the CPU on a 12-thread
  PC, even when nothing was happening. That's now about 0.5% while it's listening.
- While it's waiting for a game, it closes its audio streams completely and uses nothing.

### A tidier settings window
- **Two new pages.** Games has its own page, and **Shortcuts** gathers the pause hotkey, voice commands and the
  controller button. General and Clipping are much shorter for it.
- **Search.** Type in the box at the top of the sidebar (or press Ctrl+F) to jump straight to a setting.
- **Keyboard.** Tab moves through every control with a clear focus ring, arrows change sliders and pages, and
  Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 switch pages. Screen readers now get a name for every control.
- On Windows 11 the sidebar is translucent, like Windows' own Settings.

### History
- Clips are grouped by day, each with a thumbnail.
- Filter the list: All, Saved, Didn't save or Not funny.
- A clip that didn't save says why, with a **Test Clip** button to check it's working again.

### Smoother
- Animations now run at your monitor's refresh rate, so they're as smooth on a 144 Hz screen as on a 60 Hz one.
- Changing page slides and fades, the sidebar highlight glides between pages, and the History numbers count up.
- A new popup grows out of the one already on screen instead of replacing it abruptly.
- If you've turned off **Animation effects** in Windows, Laugh Clipper keeps things still too.

### Detection, double-checked
- Surround-sound devices (5.1, 7.1) were heard much quieter than they are; they're now mixed down properly.
- Checked every sound the app listens for against the sound model, and that quiet microphones score the same as
  loud ones.

Your settings carry over. Nothing was removed, some of it just moved pages.
