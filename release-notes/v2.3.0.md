## What's new

### See what it's doing at a glance
- The top of **General** is now one card: whether it's listening, which game is in front, how many sources it
  hears and whether Medal is ready. Anything in the way is listed right under it.
- On **Detection**, the live score stays at the top while you scroll, so you can watch it react to whatever you're
  changing. The three timing sliders most people never touch now sit behind **More timing**.

### A setup guide that shows you the popup
- The first step shows the real popup that will appear over your game, and the last step ends on "Clip saved".

### Choose your voice phrases
- On **Shortcuts**, each hype phrase has its own switch. Keep "clip that" and turn off "oh my god", for example.
  A phrase that's off isn't listened for at all.

### History
- Click a clip's thumbnail to play it. Each row now has one button, **Show in Folder**.
- A clip that arrives while you're looking at the list is pointed out, where the list used to jump.

### Smoother, and calmer
- Changing page with the keyboard (Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8, the arrow keys, search) is now instant. Clicking still slides.
- The page change is quicker.
- History numbers only count up when they change, not every time you open the page.
- Buttons press down when you click them, like the other controls.
- With Windows' **Animation effects** off, pages still fade gently where they used to snap.

### Tidier
- Shorter help text on several rows.
- **What's New** leads with the main changes and keeps the rest behind **Show All Changes**.

Your settings carry over.
