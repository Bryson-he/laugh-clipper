## What's new

### Trigger on more than laughs
- New **Trigger on** section on the Detection page: Laughter, Cheering, Shouting, Screaming and Gasp.
  Turn on any mix. Laughter stays the only one on until you change it.
- The popup says what it heard ("Cheering detected", "Saving clip · Still shouting"), with a surprised
  face for shouts, screams and gasps.
- The Audio page's "Hearing:" line shows which sound scored.

### Only listen while playing
- New **Games** section on the General page. Turn on **Only listen while playing** and detection pauses
  whenever no game is the window in front, so videos and music between games don't set it off.
- Games are found automatically: anything full-screen counts, and games from Steam, Epic, Xbox, Riot,
  EA, Ubisoft and GOG are added to **Your games** the first time you play them. Add others with
  **Add Game…** (pick a running app or browse for the exe), and remove any you don't want.
- It keeps listening for 5 seconds after you alt-tab, and resumes instantly when you go back.
- The sidebar, tray icon tooltip and tray menu show "Waiting for a game" or the game you're playing.

### Warns when Medal isn't running
- If Medal is closed, the popup now says "Medal isn't running, so no clip was saved" instead of
  pretending to save it, and no hotkey is sent.
- The General page shows a warning with an **Open Medal** button, and the tray shows it too.
- You can turn the warning off on the Clipping page.

### Fixes
- Subtitles on turned-off settings now dim along with their titles.

Your existing settings carry over. All new options start in a way that keeps the app working exactly
as before until you turn them on.
