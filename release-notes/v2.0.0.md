## What's new

### Say "don't clip"
- Turn on **Clipping → Cancel with your voice**, then while a popup is up just say **"don't clip"** (or "do not clip",
  or "cancel clip") to call it off, even in the middle of an auto-save countdown.
- Speech recognition runs offline on your PC. It only listens for those words, only on your mic, and only while a
  popup is showing. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

### "Not funny" button
- Every popup now has a 👎 button. Press it and the clip is skipped, and the trigger level for that sound goes up a
  little, so the same kind of noise is less likely to set it off again.
- Skipped moments show up in History as "Not funny". You can set the level back on the Detection page any time.

### Pause hotkey
- Pick a key on **General → Pause hotkey** to pause and resume listening from inside a game. A small popup confirms
  which way it went.

### History stats and Play
- The History page now shows your clips saved, this week's count, your most clipped game and your best laugh score.
- Each saved clip has a **Play** button that opens it in your video player, next to **Show** for its folder.

### Website
- Laugh Clipper has a homepage at **bryson-he.github.io/laugh-clipper**, to share with friends.

## Improvements
- The popup is a little wider so long game and source names fit, and gets a new compact style for quick messages
  (cancelled, paused, resumed).
- Hotkeys read more naturally everywhere, like **Ctrl + Alt + P** instead of "Ctrl + ALT + P".
- Tidier code under the hood: windows (settings, setup guide, calibration, What's new) are opened and closed the same
  way, and the hotkeys are registered together.
- Fresh screenshots in the README.

Your settings carry over, and the new options are off until you turn them on.
