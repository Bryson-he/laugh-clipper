## What's new

### Group laughs
- Turn on **Detection → Group laughs**. When you and your friends (or the game) crack up at the same time, it triggers
  sooner and at a lower level than one person laughing.
- The popup says **Group laugh** and shows who was laughing, like "You + Voice Chat".
- It needs your mic and at least one PC audio source, like Voice Chat, turned on.

### "Clip that" and hype phrases
- Turn on **Clipping → Clip with hype phrases**, then say **"clip that"** to save a clip straight away.
- Shout **"no way"**, **"oh my god"** or **"let's go"** and it counts as a moment, just like a laugh. The popup shows
  what you said.
- Hype phrases only count when you say them on their own, so "no wait, come back" doesn't set it off.
- Like "don't clip", it all runs offline on your PC. Nothing you say leaves it.

### Clip with a controller or wheel
- On the **Clipping** page, click **Clip button** and press any button on an Xbox or PlayStation controller, a racing
  wheel or a button box.
- Pressing it saves the clip while a popup is up, or any time if you pick **Works → Any time**.
- It works while the game is in front, with nothing extra to install.

## Improvements
- The popup drops "to clip" when a long source name wouldn't fit.
- The website's feature list covers the new features.

Your settings carry over, and the new options are off until you turn them on.
