What it does while you play

It sits in your tray, leaves your game in focus, and only speaks up when something's funny.

  • Hears more than laughs

    Laughter, cheering, shouting, screaming or gasps, each with its own level and sources.

  • Only while you play

    Rests when no game is in front, and adds your Steam, Epic, Xbox and Riot games by itself.

  • Knows it saved

    Checks Medal really made the clip, and keeps a history of every one with a thumbnail.

  • “Clip that”

    Say it to save a clip, shout “no way” to count a moment, or say “don’t clip” to call it off. It all runs offline, and you choose which phrases it listens for.

  • Group laughs

    When you and your friends crack up together, it triggers sooner. The whole lobby losing it is always a clip.

  • Controller or wheel

    Save the clip with any button on your pad, racing wheel or button box while you stay in the game.

  • Bookmarks recordings

    Recording a whole session in Medal? Each laugh drops a bookmark into the recording.

  • Longer laughs, longer clips

    Uses Medal’s 60 s or 120 s clip when the laughing goes on, so the whole moment fits.

Asks first, or saves it for you

A small capsule pops up over your game. One click (or your Medal hotkey) and it's saved. With auto-save on, it waits until the laughing stops so the whole moment makes it in.

  • Your game keeps keyboard and mouse focus
  • “Not funny” teaches it to be stricter
  • Optional chime for exclusive-fullscreen games

Tune it to your voice in a minute

A live score stays at the top while you change anything, showing how much each sound resembles a laugh. Or let Calibrate listen to your game and your laughs and pick the level for you.

Hears your mic, game and friends separately

Listen to your microphone and any of your PC's outputs at once. With Elgato Wave Link every channel shows up on its own, so you can follow the game and voice chat and ignore your music.

Every clip, in one place

History groups your clips by day with a thumbnail, what was heard, where and in which game. Click a thumbnail to play it.

Up and running in a minute

A short setup guide walks you through it the first time.

  1. Install it

    Download the installer and run it. It needs no admin rights, and it updates itself.

  2. Match Medal

    It reads your clip and bookmark hotkeys straight from Medal. You can also press them yourself.

  3. Laugh once

    Laugh for a few seconds and it suggests the right trigger level for your voice.

Questions

The short answers. There are more in the README.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT licence.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

The installer has no code signature yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Smart App Control blocked it.

Smart App Control blocks every unsigned app and offers no override. To use Laugh Clipper, turn it off in Windows Security → App & browser control → Smart App Control settings. On most PCs, turning it back on means resetting Windows.

Does it record or upload my audio?

No. Audio is analysed on your PC a second at a time and thrown away. The speech model for voice commands runs offline too. The only thing it connects to is GitHub, to check for updates.

Do I need Medal?

Yes. Medal does the recording. Laugh Clipper presses Medal's hotkey for you at the right moment, and warns you when Medal is closed.

The popup doesn't show over my game.

Windows keeps everything else off the screen during exclusive-fullscreen games. Use borderless fullscreen, or turn on the notification sound.
